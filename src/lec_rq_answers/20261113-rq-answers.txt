Pick 3 types of memory allocators and list 1 pro and con for each. 

For example: 

First Fit
Pro: Fairly fast allocation since it stops searching as soon as it finds a large
     enough block.
Con: Can lead to more fragmentation by leaving small, unusable gaps in the
     memory.

Best Fit 
Pro: More memory efficient as it tightly packs data into small suitable blocks
     of memory.
Con: Slower allocation because it must potentially scan the entire heap to find 
     an optimally sized block of memory. 

Bump Allocator
Pro: Very fast allocation (O(1)) because you only need to advance a pointer and 
     allocate at the end of the heap. 
Con: Cannot free individual allocations.
