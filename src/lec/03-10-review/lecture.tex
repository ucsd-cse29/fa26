\texttt{realloc} wraps malloc + memcpy + free into one call:

\begin{lstlisting}[basicstyle=\ttfamily\footnotesize, numbers=none]
void *realloc(void *ptr, size_t new_size);
\end{lstlisting}

\vspace{-0.5em}

\begin{itemize} \itemsep0em
  \item May extend in place or allocate + copy + free
  \item \texttt{realloc(NULL, n)} acts like \texttt{malloc(n)}
\end{itemize}
